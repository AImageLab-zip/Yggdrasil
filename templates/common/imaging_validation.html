Phase 3 validation harness

Phase 3 replaces the CBCT and brain volume grid with Cornerstone3D and deletes NiiVue. There are no feature flags, so this run is the safety net: it loads each volume three times — once through Cornerstone, once through NiiVue 0.69, and once straight into the NIfTI reader — and compares both viewers against the file’s own affine rather than against each other.

Tier 1 (geometry) and Tier 2 (intensity) are gates. Tier 3 (appearance) is human-reviewed and never a gate. Volumes are large and are held in GPU memory twice at a time, so keep this tab open and in the foreground.
